FAQ

Questions, answered

If yours is not here, the Discord is the fastest place to ask.

Is Mirai Launcher free?

Yes. It is free and open source under GPL-3.0. There are no ads, no paid tiers, no premium renderer unlocks and no in-app purchases.

Do I need to own Minecraft?

Yes, if you want to play online with a real account. Mirai does not include the game and does not give you an account.

  • Microsoft account — for Java Edition you own, including online servers.
  • Offline account — a local username, good for single-player and LAN. No purchase needed, and you can equip a skin and cape on it.
  • Yggdrasil / auth servers — for third-party servers running their own authentication.
Which APK should I download?

Almost every modern phone is arm64-v8a. If your device is older or unusual, the all build runs on any of the four supported architectures — it is just a bigger download.

The four ABIs are armeabi-v7a (32-bit ARM), arm64-v8a (64-bit ARM), x86 and x86_64.

What are the requirements?

Android 8.0 or newer. 4 GB of RAM is a realistic floor for a modern Minecraft version with mods; more headroom helps. Budget a couple of gigabytes of free storage for the launcher's Java runtime, on top of whatever your versions and modpacks need.

Why does the first launch take so long?

Mirai unpacks its bundled Java runtime and components on first start, and it looks like nothing is happening while it does. This is a one-time cost — later launches are much quicker.

The game crashed, or will not start at all. What now?

Check the renderer first, in Version settings → Renderer. It is the most common cause by a wide margin.

  • On 1.16.5 or older, try LTW Legacy, then GL4ES, then vgpu.
  • On 1.17 or newer, try LTW, then MobileGlues, then Zink.

If you have Sodium, Embedium or Rubidium installed and the game dies as you join a world, update Mirai — recent builds patch those mods' configs to a crash-free chunk backend on every renderer.

Otherwise, open the log viewer from the game screen. Mirai's crash reader usually names the culprit.

Can I use mods, shaders and resource packs?

Yes. Modpacks, mods, resource packs, shaders and world saves can all be installed from Discover, or imported from a file.

Fabric, Quilt, Legacy Fabric, Forge, NeoForge, Cleanroom and OptiFine are all supported as loaders.

Can I play on servers?

Yes. There is a server list screen, and a multiplayer screen for Terracotta-based play. Joining an online-mode server needs a Microsoft account that owns Java Edition.

Does Mirai collect any data about me?

No. There is no analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the build, not even a disabled one. Accounts, skins, capes, worlds and settings stay on your device unless you pick a login that needs the network.

Downloads and searches talk to their content sources — Modrinth, CurseForge and mirrors — because that is how they get the files. There is no telemetry alongside them.

Where do my worlds and settings live?

Inside the launcher's own storage, in per-instance directories. Version isolation is on by default, so two instances of the same Minecraft version keep separate mods, configs and worlds instead of fighting over one folder.

The built-in file manager can browse all of it, including configs, mods, worlds and backups, without leaving the app.

How do I update?

Mirai checks GitHub Releases on startup, and you can check manually from Settings → About. It compares your build number against the published manifest and points you at the right APK for your device.

Debug builds from CI are for testing. Release builds are the ones to keep.

What is Mirai, and how is it related to Zalith Launcher?

Mirai Launcher is a fork of Zalith Launcher 2 by MovTery, which is itself a well-regarded launcher. Mirai builds on that foundation under GPL-3.0.

What Mirai changes: the module and product naming, from-source builds of the LTW and LTW Legacy renderers with verification in CI, automatic renderer selection wired into the launch path, the mobile performance tools, the interface work, the 20 built-in wallpapers, and the Discover upgrades.

The upstream copyright notices are kept in place, as the licence requires.

Can I help translate, or contribute?

Yes. The launcher ships 18 languages, and more are welcome.

  • Bugs — open an issue on GitHub. A log and your device model make it fixable.
  • Feature suggestions — raise them in the Discord channel, where they can be discussed before they turn into work.
  • Translations — the fastest way to get a language fixed or added is to say so in Discord.

The source is on GitHub.

Is my device supported?

If it runs Android 8.0 or newer on one of the four supported ABIs — which covers essentially every phone from the last several years — then yes.

Renderers that need Vulkan, like Zink, depend on your GPU driver rather than on Mirai. When one does not work, another wrapper in the list usually will.
